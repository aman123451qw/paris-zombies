# Style board: Place de la République

Source frames: `frames/sheet_*.jpg`, taken from footage of the 17 Oct 2026 march (see `../footage/SUMMARY.md`). Reference only. No people from the footage go into the game.

## Map
- **Arena:** Place de la République. The Marianne monument (bronze statue on a stone plinth with a bronze lion) sits at the centre. Rows of plane trees, a wide paved plaza, metro entrances on the edges.
- **Side lanes (bought open with points):** narrow 19th-century stone streets like rue Yves Toudic, with tall limestone walls, tall windows and tight sidewalks.
- **Skyline:** zinc rooftops, terracotta chimney pots, the Eiffel Tower lit in the far distance (artistic licence; it isn't visible from République).

## Recurring visual elements (from footage)
- Cream and tan limestone Haussmann facades with wrought-iron balconies and grey zinc mansard roofs with dormers
- Terracotta chimney pots on the rooftops
- Rows of plane trees, with yellowing leaves in autumn
- Shopfronts: supermarket, phone repair shop ("Happy Phone"-style yellow sign), gold-buying shop, computer shop. Use made-up names, no real brands.
- Metal roller shutters covered in graffiti tags, some smashed shop glass
- Scattered debris: paper, bottles, broken boards, a knocked-over Vélib
- Burnt-out vans and cars (blackened, scenery only), plus lingering smoke
- Crowd barriers and pallets stacked into barricades
- Blue Paris street-name plaques, green Paris bins, "potelet" bollards

## Lighting and palette
- **Time of day:** night, the "morning after the strike" mood, with smoke haze still hanging around
- **Key light:** warm sodium orange from the streetlights (#F2A24A)
- **Fill:** cold blue night (#1E2A44)
- **Accents:** ember red and orange from burning wrecks (#E2512B); the red, white and blue of the Marianne flags (used sparingly)
- **Stone:** #D8C7A6 / #B9A27E; **zinc roof:** #6E7378; **asphalt:** wet and dark, picking up the orange reflections

## Art direction
- Stylised realism: about 80% of the way to real, chunky and readable. Think Black Ops Zombies maps, not a photoscan.
- Moody, dense fog that hides the edges of the map and keeps the polygon budget down.
- Humour lives in the zombies and props, not the environment: the environment plays it straight, the zombies are the joke.

## Hard rules
- No real people, protesters or police in any asset
- No real slogans or political text: graffiti is made-up tags only
- No real brand logos on the shopfronts
